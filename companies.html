07Questions

Before you write.

How far ahead should we write?

As soon as you know, even for the coming weekend. A group or a seminar needs several weeks, more in high season.

How many guests can you host?

Most requests are for two to twenty guests. Beyond that, tell us early and we will say honestly whether we are the right team.

Do you work outside Paris?

Yes. The Alps in winter, the Riviera in summer, elsewhere in Europe on request.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Discretion is written into our terms, which apply as soon as you accept a proposal. The identity of your guests, their programme and the name of your company are never shared without your written permission.

Can guests have particular requirements?

Kosher or halal tables, allergies, reduced mobility, security: tell us in the brief and the proposal is built around them.
